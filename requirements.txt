
SHREE TEXFAB CREATIONS PRIVATE LIMITED

Website Development Brief, Content & Information Architecture

Website Type: Static Corporate / B2B Textile Website
Primary Audience: Domestic textile buyers, wholesalers, distributors, garment manufacturers, uniform suppliers, institutional buyers, exporters, buying houses and international fabric buyers
Primary Geography: India + International Markets
Suggested Domain: shreetexfab.in
Website Language: English
Recommended Design: Premium, minimal, corporate, textile-focused
CMS: Optional. Since the website is static, CMS is not required initially.



1. WEBSITE OBJECTIVE

The purpose of the website is to establish Shree Texfab Creations Private Limited as a:

Reliable Indian textile manufacturing and sourcing partner offering quality suiting, shirting, uniform and specialty fabrics for domestic and international buyers.

The website should NOT look like an IndiaMART listing or a small textile trading company’s website.

It should communicate:

Professionalism
Textile expertise
Manufacturing and processing capability
Product diversity
Quality control
Reliability
Custom development capability
Domestic and export readiness
Long-term B2B relationships
The website should primarily generate business enquiries, rather than online sales.



2. CORE BRAND POSITIONING

Recommended positioning statement

“Fabrics That Perform. Partnerships That Last.”

Alternative:

“Indian Textile Expertise. Reliable Fabric Solutions.”

Alternative:

“From Fibre to Finished Fabric — Built Around Your Requirement.”

Recommended primary tagline:

FABRICS THAT PERFORM. PARTNERSHIPS THAT LAST.

Supporting statement:

Shree Texfab Creations Private Limited is an Indian textile company engaged in the manufacturing, sourcing, processing and supply of quality fabrics for suiting, shirting, uniforms, institutions and specialty applications.



3. IMPORTANT POSITIONING DECISION

The company should not falsely present itself as a fully vertically integrated textile mill if weaving, dyeing or processing is carried out through specialized partner/job-work units.

Instead, the website should communicate the strength of the company’s integrated sourcing, manufacturing coordination and quality-control network.

Suggested language:

“Our manufacturing and processing ecosystem brings together specialized weaving, dyeing, finishing and processing partners under coordinated quality and production management.”

This is actually a strength for a B2B buyer because it allows the company to offer:

Multiple fabric constructions
Multiple blends
Flexible quantities
Custom developments
Different finishing requirements
Competitive pricing
Faster sourcing
Specialized production

For the first version, I recommend not trying to show all 100+ qualities/products individually.

Build the corporate website around 6–8 strong product categories, and then create a downloadable PDF catalogue.

The website’s job is:

Build trust → demonstrate capability → communicate product range → generate enquiries.

The catalogue’s job is:

Show detailed qualities → technical specifications → colours → constructions → applications.

This combination will be much more effective for Shree Texfab than trying to turn the static website into a full product database.
